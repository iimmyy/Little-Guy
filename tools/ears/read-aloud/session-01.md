# Read-aloud session 01

Sixty short lines, about five minutes of reading. Each line gives his ears an exact answer (the script),
and each effect copy gives them a known difficulty.

## Recording (one take)

1. Record at 48 kHz, mono, in FL with nothing on the mixer track and nothing on the master (no limiter, no EQ).
2. Read every line in order in one take, in your normal voice. Leave about **2 seconds of silence** between lines.
   Don't say the line numbers.
3. Flubbed a line? Pause, read it again, and keep going. Write the line number down (for example "redo: 12, 30").
4. Export the take as `s01_dry.wav`.

## Effect copies (as many as you like)

Run the same take through effects and export each version as a separate file, full length, starting at the same
point as the dry take. Name each one with the effect and how much:

- `s01_reverb-heavy.wav`
- `s01_distortion-light.wav`
- `s01_beat-medium.wav` (a beat of yours underneath; the amount = how loud the beat is)
- Chains are fine: `s01_reverb-medium+bitcrush-light.wav`

Use light / medium / heavy for the amount, or a number if you prefer (`reverb-40`). The file name is the log, so
nothing else needs writing down. Mix it up: reverb, delay, distortion, bitcrush, telephone or lowpass, chorus,
pitch shift, autotune, a beat underneath, crowd noise, whatever your plugins have.

**One rule:** nothing that changes the length or timing of the take (time-stretch, speed-up, slowing down).
Pitch shift is fine as long as the length stays the same. My tool lines every copy up with the dry take and
cuts them all in the same places.

Drop the files plus your redo note in a folder and tell me where. Your voice stays private: these recordings never
go in git or anywhere public.
